Super Mario Galaxy for PS Vita — Version 3.0
============================================

Thanks for playing Super Mario Galaxy natively on PlayStation Vita! Version 3.0
brings full-resolution 60 FPS across most of the game, hardware 2x MSAA in
Quality 30 mode, instant ~0.4s shader loading (5,401 precompiled shaders), and
the first-ever Super Mario Galaxy multiplayer for local Wi-Fi and online play.

What You Need
-------------
- A PS Vita with homebrew (VitaShell)
- About 2.5 GB free on your memory card
- Your own Super Mario Galaxy disc image (Wii, USA, RMGE01)
- libshacccg.suprx set up on the Vita (once)
- Strongly recommended for best 60 FPS performance:
  * CapUnlocker plug-in (unlocks the Vita's 4th CPU core)
  * PSVshell / PSVshellPlus (overclock to 500 MHz CPU / 222 MHz GPU / 222 MHz BUS)

Easy Install (Recommended)
--------------------------
Use CBOC Vita Installer: pick Super Mario Galaxy, choose your disc image, and
click Install. https://github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/vita-installer/releases/latest

Manual Install
--------------
1. Prepare your game files from your disc image (MANUAL-PREPARE-GAME.md).
   Shrinking cutscene movies is optional and only saves card space.
2. Copy the prepared files to ux0:data/smg/game/ on your Vita.
3. Copy Galaxy-Vita.vpk to your Vita.
4. Install it in VitaShell and launch from the LiveArea.

Updating from 2.x
-----------------
Install the new 3.0 VPK right over your old one. Your saves and settings stay
intact. If LiveArea still shows the old look, run Refresh LiveArea in VitaShell.
