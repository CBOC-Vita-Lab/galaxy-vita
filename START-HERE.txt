Super Mario Galaxy Vita 1.0
===========================

Thanks for trying Super Mario Galaxy on your PS Vita. It runs natively, not in an
emulator, and it was built to feel at home on the Vita: touch to point, rear
touch to aim, tilt to steer, and a button to spin.

What You Need
-------------
- A PS Vita with homebrew (VitaShell)
- About 2.5 GB free on the memory card
- Your own Super Mario Galaxy disc image (Wii, USA, RMGE01)
- libshacccg.suprx set up on the Vita (once)

Easy Install
------------
Use CBOC Vita Installer: pick Super Mario Galaxy, choose your disc image,
press Install. https://github.com/CBOC-Vita-Lab/vita-installer/releases/latest

Manual Install
--------------
1. Prepare your game files from your disc image, and shrink the movies
   so they play smoothly (MANUAL-PREPARE-GAME.md, steps 1 to 3).
2. Copy them to ux0:data/smg/game/ on the Vita.
3. Copy Galaxy-Vita.vpk to the Vita.
4. Install it in VitaShell and launch the bubble.

Updating
--------
Install the new VPK over the old one. Your saves and settings stay.
